SAFARIYA ADVENTURE

Privacy Notice.

Effective 21 October 2026

Information you provide

When account services are configured, SAFARIYA uses your name and email to create and secure your account. If you send a trip or change request, the details you provide (such as preferred dates, traveller count, phone number and notes) are stored to respond to that request.

How information is handled

Account authentication and customer request records are designed to use Supabase. Customer records are restricted by database row-level security to the authenticated account that owns them. The website does not store card numbers and does not currently process payments.

Retention and requests

Records may remain in the service while needed to manage your account and travel requests. To ask about, correct or delete your information, contact hello@safariya.travel.

External services

Sign-in email and social authentication, when enabled, are handled by Supabase and the selected identity provider. Their service terms and privacy notices also apply.

This notice describes the current website functionality and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
